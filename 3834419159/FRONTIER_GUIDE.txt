Frontier colonization — Warhammer Fantasy CK3 1.4.0

Start a NEW CAMPAIGN. Existing map saves are not supported for this expansion.

Decisions > Fund a Frontier Expedition opens a scrollable county picker.
Choose neighbouring wilderness or a reachable coast. Established coastal
colonies can become new bases for later expeditions.

Initial funding: 150 gold. Supplies: 6 gold every 90 days in both phases.
Expeditions usually take about 2–3 years; outposts generally need another
7–10 years. Terrain and natural racial suitability adjust progress mildly.
AI rulers progress at 80% speed, need at least 500 gold and five realm
counties, and face a 12-year preparation cooldown. A ruler cannot begin
another expedition while sponsoring an unfinished project.

Manage Frontier Settlement displays phase progress and supply information.
It also allows withdrawal while at peace. Eight consecutive unpaid quarters
return the site to wilderness. Project ownership follows the sponsoring
title and then the outpost county, so a successor inherits the obligation.

Frontier Wars can seize developing outposts. Ten points of progress are
lost to fighting; the winner inherits the supply bill. Occupation pauses
development. Established settlements use a normal holding suited to their
government and can be retained or granted like other counties. Frontier
duchies become formable once their region has established settlements,
subject to the game's normal creation conditions.

Wilderness represents unsettled country, not necessarily empty country.
The inert Wilderness Custodian is an engine placeholder, not a playable
faction. Real impassable mountain barriers remain closed.

The update restores 418 source provinces as 196 frontier counties with
222 empty future barony slots. Province IDs and map shapes are preserved.
Human multiplayer and long-campaign balance testing are still recommended.

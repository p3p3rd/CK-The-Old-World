[h1]Warhammer Fantasy CK3[/h1]

Rule the world of Warhammer Fantasy through Crusader Kings III: build dynasties, master the Winds of Magic, lead the armies of the Old World and beyond, and carve a lasting realm from a dangerous frontier.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A full Warhammer world map, covering the Old World, Ulthuan, Naggaroth, Lustria, the Southlands, Cathay and the lands beyond.
[*]A 2502 IC campaign with regional bookmarks, featured rulers and lore-based important families. Scenario backgrounds use the CK3 realm map and terrain, while the scenario buttons retain their themed illustrations.
[*]Counties based on EU4 states, with multiple baronies where geography allows. Developed centres, frontier provinces, holding tiers and empty construction slots offer different starting opportunities.
[*]Original low-poly racial portraits, distinct regional human appearances, species traits, lifespans, strengths and weaknesses. The portrait system uses fixed-pose models rather than full vanilla facial animation.
[*]Lore-based culture groups, differentiated culture colours, faiths, religious symbols, holy sites and a range of governments suited to the realms.
[*]Separate Warhammer magic lifestyles with branching perk paths, lore and racial restrictions, and a dedicated Dwarf runesmithing path.
[*]A visible mana resource and an Arcane Codex with lore selection, favourites and spell management. Sustain boons, wards and hostile effects by paying mana upkeep; dismiss or dispel channels as circumstances change.
[*]Faction troops, monsters, dragons and war engines with custom 2D illustrations, differentiated regiment sizes, terrain effects and counters. Starting innovations reflect major settled powers and their strengths.
[*]Monuments, special mines and religious sites drawn from the setting, alongside weapons, armour, trinkets, books, regalia and court artifacts to acquire through play.
[*]Formable empires and hegemonies earned through regional conquest and thematic goals, with defined de jure heartlands moving when the titles are formed.
[*]Earned honours and traits such as Grail Knight, Slayer, Warboss, Chaos marks and Celestial Dragon, plus a diplomatic alliance action for rulers with sufficiently good relations and diplomatic reach.
[*]Frontier colonization: 418 formerly impassable source provinces restored as 196 wilderness counties. Fund land or coastal expeditions, establish outposts and support them until they become normal holdings.
[*]Settlement progress, supply upkeep, mild race and terrain effects, cautious slower AI expansion, succession-aware projects, abandonment after prolonged shortages, and dedicated Frontier Wars over unfinished outposts.
[*]Twelve original low-poly loading illustrations covering iconic Warhammer scenes, replacing the installed vanilla and DLC loading artworks.
[*]Optional companion mods for the Iron Sultanate, alternative portraits, landmark scenery, heraldry and named legendary artifacts.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements and setup[/h2]
Requires Crusader Kings III 1.20.*. Start a [b]new campaign[/b] for the frontier map update. Enable this conversion first, followed by the optional submods you want to use. Other total conversions should be disabled.

Colonization is found under [b]Decisions → Fund a Frontier Expedition[/b]. Use [b]Manage Frontier Settlement[/b] to inspect an active project or withdraw support. Initial funding costs 150 gold; supplies cost 6 gold every 90 days. The optional Artifacts pack adds its own named-relic catalogue and quests.

[h2]Human/AI collaboration[/h2]
This is a human/AI collaborative fan project. AI has assisted with parts of the code and artwork; it was not used for everything. Creative direction, selection, review and playtesting are human-led.

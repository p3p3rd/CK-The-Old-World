Warhammer Fantasy CK3 is a free, unofficial fan-made total conversion for Crusader Kings III. It is an independent community project with no affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement from the rights holders.

Warhammer Fantasy, its setting, characters, and associated marks belong to Games Workshop and its licensors. Thank you to the Games Workshop writers, artists, and designers who created the Old World.

Special thanks to JeffGomez (jeff.gomez) and Nuka_Bear (nuka_bear) of Warhammer Fantasy Universalis. Nuka_Bear made the source map and most of the culture grouping. The map and culture and religion setup were adapted from their Europa Universalis IV project with the authors’ permission. Thanks also to CupcakesArePain, Doomedworld, Taihno, Kaladin23, and the other Warhammer Fantasy Universalis contributors.
